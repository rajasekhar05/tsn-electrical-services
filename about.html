About TSN Electrical & Service

A supplier and service support partner for mechanical, electrical, industrial tools and maintenance consumables.

Who We Are

One vendor for the full maintenance shopping list.

TSN Electrical and Service Private Limited works as a supply and service support partner covering mechanical, electrical, industrial tools and maintenance consumables. Instead of splitting requirements across separate vendors, plants and facilities teams route bearings, seals, cabling, switchgear and tooling requirements through a single point of contact.

We are based out of Bellary, Karnataka, and support requirements ranging from routine stock replenishment to urgent breakdown spares.
